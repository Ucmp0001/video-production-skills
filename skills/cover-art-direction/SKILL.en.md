# Cover art direction

[中文 / canonical entry](SKILL.md)

Treat the cover as a dedicated design task. Gather stable copy, the title/core conflict, source qualifiers, target platforms, authorized assets, brand rules and real reference images. Do not casually substitute a video screenshot for a designed proposal.

## Design
Use the [cover brief](references/cover-brief.en.md), actually open references and record the scope observed. State if the reference library is empty or unreadable. Learn visible hierarchy, relationships, photography and negative space without copying accounts, Logos or unverified claims.

Distill one IDEA: who/what is the subject, what relationship changes, and why watch? Propose a few clearly different directions under the same factual promise, comparing photography, space, material, action and typography. Do not require imitation of a particular brand or palette.

The main headline should normally explain the topic and conflict/reason to watch, with a short subtitle or object labels when needed. Make it understandable before shortening; no universal character limit applies. Large type, black/white and controlled bright accents are not inherently poor design. Revise overload, unclear subjects and undeliverable exaggeration.

## Produce
When the user specifies a creator/model, explicitly supply necessary content, readable images and output interfaces, then verify actual artifacts. If unavailable, mark design pending rather than silently impersonating that creator.

Generate/select the main visual, then typeset separately. Check every character, punctuation, attribution and qualifier, especially Chinese text. Save a text-free master for visual inspection; publication versions normally retain headlines unless the brand explicitly calls for no title.

Recompose and typeset each requested ratio; cropping alone is not adaptation. Use 9:16, 3:4, 1:1 or 16:9 according to actual destinations. Inspect phone-sized thumbnails for focus, hierarchy, subject recognition, edges and platform overlays.

## Independent review and delivery
After self-checking, give an independent reviewer or isolated review context the actual master and exports, content payoff locations, sources, references and current hashes. Without independent observation, do not self-certify independent approval.

Image, text or ratio changes invalidate affected previous reviews. Limit revision rounds if appropriate; unresolved failures remain cover-pending rather than triggering endless image generation.

Deliver concept and selection rationale, final copy, text-free master, editable typography, target-ratio exports, asset provenance/rights and version manifest. Script/research-only tasks need no premature cover production.
