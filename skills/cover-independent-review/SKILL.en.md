# Independent cover review

[中文 / canonical entry](SKILL.md)

Use a reviewer outside the design task or a fresh isolated review context. A different context does not necessarily mean a different model; record actual roles and models. The designer's own summary is not an independent report.

## Inputs and observation
Obtain current copy, payoff locations, necessary sources/qualifiers, brand rules, actual references, text-free master, publication exports and hashes. Actually open images with visual capability. Paths, text and model descriptions alone permit textual checks only; visual acceptance remains pending.

Use the [review template](references/cover-review.en.md). First state who/what is visible, what happens and why watch. Then inspect phone-sized thumbnails for:
1. One clear subject and core relationship without scattered focus.
2. Visual quality, believable proportions, hands/faces/object structure and generation artifacts.
3. Complete headline, necessary attribution and body payoff; “proposed” must not become “completed.”
4. Glyphs, complete Chinese text, hierarchy, contrast, wrapping and small-size readability.
5. Information load: title, labels and decoration should not compete.
6. Space, edges, platform safe areas, human crops and branding.
7. Real recomposition across ratios without mechanically cutting subjects or text.
8. Media rights and generated nature; attribution or realism alone does not prove permission.

Large titles, opposing subjects, black/white with bright accents and fuller copy can work. Avoid rigid aesthetic bans. Ground judgments in actual files, problem locations, impact and minimal fixes.

## Conclusions and invalidation
P0: core falsehood or severe unusability. P1: unclear meaning, missing qualifiers, overload or significant visual defects. P2: refinements. Close real P0/P1 issues only after fixes and inspection of new images.

Return pass, revise or observation-pending, separately listing unknown rights and publication conditions. Check current hashes. Recheck affected parts after image, title, content promise or ratio changes.

Approval covers only the recorded inspection scope and does not authorize upload/publication. Deliver the independent report, observation scope, per-ratio results, handling of significant findings and current versions.
