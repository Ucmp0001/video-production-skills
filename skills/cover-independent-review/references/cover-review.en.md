## Inputs and scope
Current copy/title version; reviewer and actual model; master/export files and hashes; images actually opened; phone display size; references; unobserved scope.

## First-glance understanding
Describe the subject, relationship and reason to watch before comparing against the script's promise. Do not depend on the designer's explanation to make an unreadable image understandable.

## Separate conclusions
Main visual, factual promise, complete text, hierarchy/load, thumbnail, recomposition, safe areas, brand and unknown rights. State pass/revise/pending and exact scope. The director cannot be the independent signer; disclose same-model separate-context review when used.

Changes to images, copy, content promise or ratio invalidate affected conclusions. Review does not replace media permissions or external-action authorization.
