# Evidence-based video scripts

[中文 / canonical entry](SKILL.md)

Start with a specific question the audience wants answered. For a person or company, build around goals, obstacles, actions, choices, costs and consequences. A mechanism explainer can start with the problem without inventing a protagonist.

## Verify the inputs
Separate established facts, attributed reporting, speculation and the author's analysis. Check current reliable sources for recent numbers, dates or disputes; retain links, publication/access dates and measurement definitions. Missing research capability means an explicit gap, not evidence supplied from model memory.

Use the [story and evidence template](references/story-evidence.en.md) to connect important claims, sources, material and the place where the finished piece answers its promise. Check that footage for the opening and key turns is obtainable early. Track factual gaps, asset gaps and pending permissions separately.

## Write
- Carry one core question throughout. The first line and first shot should pose the same answerable question. Build suspense from the available material; do not hide counterevidence needed to judge the story.
- Each paragraph adds an action, constraint, fact or explanation. Add background when it helps explain a choice. Do not invent dialogue, expressions, thoughts, scene details or motives.
- Explain jargon through products, orders, money, behavior or spatial relationships. State the scope of assumptions such as fixed capacity. Correlation alone does not establish causation.
- Use natural spoken language, varying sentence length with the information. Remove repetition, mechanical parallelism, empty summaries and forced lessons; preserve the author's position.
- Answer the opening question at the end, returning to the consequential choices. State what remains unknown where relevant.
- Label script duration as an estimate. Adjust final timing to the actual recording. Interview excerpts preserve original meaning, sound and necessary context; do not silently turn an interview into narration.

## Deliver
Provide the voiceover, claim/source mapping, opening-to-payoff map, asset gaps and necessary title options. Titles and shorter versions retain qualifiers such as “proposed,” “reportedly” and “hypothetical.”

Important public copy can receive independent review with the full script and source boundaries. Review suggestions still require verification and revision. Never claim a second review passed without an actual external report.
