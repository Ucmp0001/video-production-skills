## One through-line
Audience question; subject and goal; actual obstacle; action/choice; cost and result; closing answer. For an explainer, use the problem, constraints and change without inventing a story.

## Opening and payoff
First-frame subject/action, first sentence, change in the first three seconds, answer location, necessary qualifiers, available assets and usage rights.

Map titles and summaries to the script. Funding intentions are not closed transactions; prototypes are not mass production; hypothetical models are not real company performance.

## Self-check
Does it sound natural? Which sentence repeats information? Are motives supported? Can jargon be explained through concrete objects? Is duration only an estimate? Mark missing sources as unknown and list alternatives for missing footage. Preserve the meaning and necessary context of interviews.
