# Learn from reference videos

[中文 / canonical entry](SKILL.md)

Distinguish learning, creative design, continuation/revision and review. For learning-only requests, deliver methods/templates without automatically producing, purchasing, installing or publishing anything.

## Establish observations
Use the [observation and transfer template](references/reference-study.en.md) for source, hash, timecodes, transcript, actual frame/clip/sound observations and scope. Separate the tutorial author's statements, prompt goals, visible behavior, code-verified behavior and local tests.

Sample broadly, then inspect important actions, transitions, operations and prompt cards more closely. Images support static observations; transcripts support speech content. Without source code, on-screen labels do not prove a rendering technology. An author's performance, price or one-click claims do not become facts about this project.

## Extract reusable methods
Identify “problem → initial state → trigger → action → result → next-shot handoff.” Explain what the visuals contribute and why viewers can follow.

Describe materials, space, motion and reading rules instead of vague “premium/cinematic” labels. Hold the content promise steady while comparing options. Do not mechanically copy Logos, colors, verses, people or counts from references.

Even a short creative request needs the full relevant script, assets, brand, tool interfaces and actual timing. Explicitly send necessary context to the creator; a link or the existence of CLAUDE.md does not prove receipt.

## Apply and review
Try a complete representative event likely to reveal the weakness. Reuse the current project; do not blindly replace stacks or execute unfamiliar tutorial commands. Watch/listen at normal speed and separate technical from comprehension checks.

When an independent observer is available, first ask them to describe the topic, state change and confusion without the script, then compare with script/sources. Do not cue expected answers. Without audiovisual capability, do not claim a blind test occurred.

Record symptoms, causal evidence, minimal fixes, rechecks and applicability. Update skills only from reproducible learning; do not turn one case's timings, style counts, motion parameters or tool failure into universal rules.

Deliver a time index, concrete methods and conditions, ready-to-use briefs/checkpoints and unverified items. Route further production through the project's current stage when requested.
