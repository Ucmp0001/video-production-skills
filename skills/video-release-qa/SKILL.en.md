# Final video and publication-package QA

[中文 / canonical entry](SKILL.md)

Two modes: export review and platform packaging. If the user only wants copy, provide a draft without waiting for a cover. For a complete publication package, check every required artifact for the actual destinations.

## Review the current export
Use the [release checks](references/release-checks.en.md) with current script, captions, shots, sources, rights, project, measured parameters and cover reports. Inspect actual versions; empty checklists and old reports do not establish acceptance.

Record separately:
- Content: names, dates, values, quotations, source strength, causality, qualifiers and opening payoff.
- Technical: playback/decoding, dimensions/ratio/fps/duration, audiovisual timing and subtitle format.
- Stills and continuous motion: actual frames, normal-speed clips, event order, reading time, crops and occlusion.
- Sound: actual listening to pronunciation, delivery, joins, music balance and original context.
- Cover: design and independent observation of current exports, ratios and content promises.
- Rights/publication: specific footage, music, voice, likeness/privacy, generated provenance and current platform disclosures.

Black/freeze/silence detectors only identify candidate intervals. Watch/listen to judge them: darkness, stillness or silence may be intentional. State sampling scope without claiming full viewing. Missing playable/audible material stays pending; transcripts and waveforms do not substitute.

P0: core falsehood, severe misleading content or unusable export. P1: important omissions affecting comprehension, main promises, synchronization or delivery. P2: refinements not blocking current use. Include sentence/shot/actual time, evidence, impact, minimal fix and recheck method. Preserve actual independent reports and verified decisions. Model agreement adds no factual source. Keep unknown facts/rights explicit and never promise zero risk.

## Platform packaging
Use stable final copy, captions and specifications for the user's selected platforms. Provide each platform's title, description, necessary sources/disclosures, cover path and relevant hashtags directly at the end of the copy. Avoid identical generic bundles and unrelated trends.

Short copy, summaries and covers retain conclusions and qualifiers such as “proposed,” “reportedly” and “hypothetical.” Chapters use the final timeline. Platform limits change; verify current official rules before asserting hard limits, otherwise mark them unverified.

## Status and delivery
Call it “production package complete” only when required production checks are done. Missing audiovisual/cover checks mean “review version,” with specific intervals, files and next steps. Record publication conditions and user authorization separately; production acceptance does not mean published.

Deliver clickable paths to the video, project if needed, soft subtitles, reviewed covers, platform copy, sources and acceptance record. No ZIP by default. External actions require the user's explicit authorization; invoking this skill alone does not authorize publication.
