# Audio and caption checks

[中文](audio-captions.md)

The local fingerprint can hash provider, model, voice, expression and parameters. Never submit private voice references, logs or credentials to a public repository.

- Listen to key names, companies, quantities, money, units, dates, negation, reporting qualifiers and foreign words.
- Check joins, breaths, pauses and the complete final sentence. Expression instructions must not be spoken.
- Check music identity/permission, intelligible voice, smooth edges and inappropriate clipping; retain original tracks.
- UTF-8 SRT: numbering, boundaries, semantic splits and wrapping. Start ≥ 0; end > start. Timing must fit the actual audio.
- At phone size, inspect type, contrast, crop, safe areas, existing captions and persistent corner marks.
- After editing speech/pauses, recalculate affected captions, shots and events; preserve originals and soft subtitles.

Record file/version, actual viewing/listening scope, intervals, observer, issue, fix and recheck. Machine warnings are not listening conclusions.
